# OAAF articles

Articles about the [Operational AI Assurance Framework (OAAF)](../), and about running AI systems safely once they are live.

- [Should this AI system still be running? Introducing OAAF](introducing-oaaf.md). A long, complete introduction: why testing before launch isn't enough, the two-question test, the control states, the 36 requirements, the decision rules and three worked examples. About 35 minutes.
- [Negative-space assurance: the AI decisions nobody reviews](negative-space-assurance.md). Why oversight follows what an AI system flags rather than what it lets through, and how to check your own system in an afternoon. About 8 minutes.

Try it yourself: the [online assessment tool](../tool/) and the [Two-Assessor Challenge](../challenge/).
